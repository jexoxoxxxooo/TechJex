What I edit

Built for the format, not forced into it.

A YouTube long-form edit, a podcast, and a 30-second cut all need different pacing. The service stays flexible so the edit fits the platform.

01

Long-Form & YouTube

Process

Same four passes, every project.

The order doesn't change. It's what keeps a two-day turnaround from feeling rushed.

00:01

Ingest & Sync

Footage, audio, and any assets get organized and synced before a single cut is made.

00:02

Story Pass

A rough cut built around the strongest version of the story, not just the order things were filmed.

00:03

Sound & Color

Dialogue leveled, noise cleaned, music mixed under it, and a grade applied for a consistent look.

00:04

Deliver & Revise

Export in the format the platform needs, with a revision round built into the timeline.
